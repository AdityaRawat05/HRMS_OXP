# PeoplePay360 — HR & Payroll

An integrated Human Resource and Payroll Operations Platform for managing the complete employee lifecycle — from employee master data and attendance to leave management, payroll processing, payslips, and reporting.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

PeoplePay360 is an HR and Payroll platform designed to connect HR operations and payroll into one end-to-end workflow.

The system manages:

- Employee profiles and employment information
- Contracts and employment history
- Working schedules
- Attendance and worked hours
- Time Off / Leave requests and allocations
- Salary Structures and Salary Rules
- Payruns and Payslips
- Payroll validation and warnings
- Payslip PDF generation
- Bulk payslip email delivery
- Payroll Dashboard and reporting

The Employee record acts as the central hub connecting HR and payroll information.
